01 / About

Quality is a decision-making process.

Guideline interpretation, contextual judgment, consistent classification and actionable feedback are central to reliable AI data quality and content delivery.

My work combines hands-on language-data evaluation with functional quality leadership.

Over more than a decade in language and localization, I have reviewed high-volume content, built terminology and classification resources, evaluated machine-generated outputs, and helped teams apply shared quality standards.

At TeamWorker, I performed functional language-lead responsibilities in an enterprise telecommunications environment: training writers and linguists, reviewing deliverables, clarifying guidelines, addressing escalations, and using recurring quality issues to shape practical workshops.

My approach is evidence-led: define the acceptance criteria, inspect the work against the source and guidelines, record the defect and its impact, communicate a clear correction, and feed recurring patterns back into training and process documentation.

Transferable quality principles

  • Apply the same criteria consistently across submissions.
  • Escalate ambiguous cases rather than silently guessing.
