01 / About

Language quality is more than correctness.

It is about meaning, context, terminology, and how language works inside a product.

My work sits at the intersection of language, software localization, and AI quality.

My experience includes English-to-Brazilian Portuguese localization, machine translation post-editing, light post-editing, linguistic QA, audiovisual translation, and linguistic data workflows.

In software-related projects, I refine AI-generated interface content and assess language against the intended function, product context, terminology, and Brazilian Portuguese conventions. The objective is not to rewrite everything. It is to identify what affects meaning or usability, make the right level of intervention, and explain decisions clearly enough to support consistent quality.

Case 02 — Fidelity vs. Standardization (Ethics in Localization)
Critical Localization Accessible Captioning Terminological Governance Systemic Error Analysis

Fidelity vs. Standardization

Ethical decisions in localization and captioning — when AI and agencies treat dialects and legitimate varieties as “errors to be corrected”.

Agency AI systems frequently treat dialectal or usage variation as errors. The result: characters lose identity markers, technical content violates corporate terminology, and regional norms are ignored in favor of a supposed “standard”. Protocols grounded in translation ethics and applied linguistics interrupt this cycle.

Dialectal Erasure

“me grandfather” (Irish) “corrected” to “my grandfather”, erasing accent and cultural identity.

Terminological Violence

“Job” rendered as “trabalho” when the corporate glossary requires “processo”, breaking consistency.

Normative Imperialism

“travelling” (EN-UK) “corrected” to “traveling” (EN-US), ignoring context and audience.

Five-Stage Analysis Protocol

1
Marker Identification

Detection of dialectal variation, corporate terminology and regional norms.

2
Ethical Classification

Distinction between a “real error” and an “identity marker to be preserved”.

3
Protocol Application

Dialect tagging, glossary lookup, variant detection.

4
Decision Documentation

Record of the applied criterion (dialectal preservation, terminological compliance, normative adequacy).

5
Structured Feedback

Argumentation for agencies and project managers grounded in linguistic theory.

05 / Work process

Intervene at the right level.

Good post-editing does not mean rewriting every sentence. It means applying the right quality criterion to the actual problem.

01

Understand context

Identify audience, product function, language variant, references, and terminology.

02

Assess output

Compare meaning, fluency, naturalness, terminology, and contextual suitability.

03

Classify issues

Separate meaning errors, terminology violations, grammar, and stylistic preferences.

04

Make the edit

Use targeted changes or deeper rewriting according to severity and purpose.

05

Explain decisions

Provide actionable feedback that supports consistency and future quality.
